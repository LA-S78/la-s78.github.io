1. Duel Schedule

Updated: June 2026

The Alliance Duel is the pulse of server activity. Use the schedule below to synchronize your resource spending and maximize rewards. Note: Days are fixed; ensure your tasks are aligned with the current phase.

Day 0 | Sunday

Prep Day
  • Before Reset: Complete but DON'T collect Falcon Quests.
  • Send lineups to Gather.
  • Save: Speedups, Recruit/Black Ops/Caravan Tickets, Raven Chests, Antitoxins, Hero Shards.

Day 1 | Monday

Enhance Raven
  • Collect Falcon Quests
  • Gather from Resource Tiles
  • Level up Raven (Consume Fruit/Essence)
  • Spend Stamina
  • Use Antitoxin (If needed)

Day 2 | Tuesday

Territory Day
  • Complete Buildings
  • DON'T collect Falcon Quests!
  • Spend Construction Speedups
  • Use UR Caravans & Black Ops Tickets
  • Recruit Survivors

Day 3 | Wednesday

Tech Research
  • Collect Falcon Quests
  • Complete Research
  • Spend Research Speedups
  • Use Study Scrolls
  • Open Raven Chests

Day 4 | Thursday

Enhance Heroes
  • Use Hero Omni Shards
  • Use Skill Badges
  • DON'T collect Falcon Quests!
  • Recruit Heroes (Tickets)

Day 5 | Friday

War Preparation
  • Collect Falcon Quests
  • Train Soldiers
  • Spend Training Speedups
  • Increase Building/Tech Might (Leftover speedups)

Day 6 | Saturday

Raid the Enemy (KE)
  • Defeat Enemy Soldiers
  • Lose Soldiers
  • Spend leftover Speedups
  • Use leftover Caravan/Black Ops Tickets
  • REMEMBER TO SHIELD UP!

2. Golden Rules

⚠️ Compliance Alert

The "Falcon" Rule: Never collect your Falcon Quests outside of the designated days. This is the single largest cause of lost potential points during Alliance Duel.
