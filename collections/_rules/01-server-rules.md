## Server Rules
The following guidelines are mandatory to ensure fair play and server stability. 

{% include rule_card.html title="📜 1. Respect & Conduct" content="
<p><strong>Zero Tolerance:</strong> Bullying, racism, hate speech, harassment, or toxic behavior is prohibited.<br>
<strong>Community Standard:</strong> Treat all players with respect.<br>
<strong>Reporting:</strong> You <strong>must</strong> provide screenshots/proof when reporting a violation.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="🛡️ 2. NAP Protection Rules" content="
<p>The following actions against <strong>NAP Alliances</strong> and their <strong>Academies</strong> are prohibited:</p>
<div style='background: rgba(180, 40, 40, 0.1); border: 1px solid #8b0000; padding: 10px; text-align: center; border-radius: 4px; color: #ff6b6b; margin-top: 10px;'>
🚫 No Attacking<br>
🚫 No Scouting
</div>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="💎 3. Resource & Map Etiquette" content="
<p><strong>Tile Safety:</strong> Attacking resource tiles is strictly forbidden. Let players farm in peace.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="🚛 4. Caravans & Black Ops" content="
<p>Governed by a <strong>Three-Strike System</strong>:</p>
<p><strong>Strike 1 & 2:</strong> Reported by R4s. Offender receives a formal warning.<br>
<strong>Strike 3:</strong> Results in a <strong>Single Base Hit</strong> penalty.<br>
<strong>Conflict Resolution:</strong> Victims may waive the strike report if an apology is accepted.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="🤝 5. Member Poaching" content="
<p><strong>Active Recruiting:</strong> Messaging members of other NAP alliances to switch is prohibited.<br>
<strong>Player Autonomy:</strong> Players are free to leave and join alliances voluntarily.<br>
<strong>Applications:</strong> 'Walk-in' applicants are allowed, provided no prior solicitation occurred.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="📉 6. Other Alliances" content="
<p><strong>Fair Play:</strong> Attacking smaller alliances because they are outside the NAP is forbidden.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="🕊️ 7. Diplomacy & Conflict Resolution" content="
<p>1. <strong>Private Resolution:</strong> Handle disputes privately between Alliance Leads/Diplomats first.<br>
2. <strong>Escalation:</strong> If unresolved, bring to <strong>NAP Leadership</strong>.</p>
<blockquote>⚠️ Do not bring rule disputes, grievances, or drama into General or World Chat. Keep it to private channels.</blockquote>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="🎓 8. Academies" content="
<p><strong>Designation:</strong> Each NAP alliance may protect <strong>one</strong> academy.<br>
<strong>Governance:</strong> Academies entering the Top 10 do not receive voting rights while they maintain academy status.</p>
" %}

{% include rule_card.html title="⚠️ 9. General Rule Violations" content="
<p><em>(Except #4)</em></p>
<p><strong>1st Offense:</strong> Official Warning.<br>
<strong>2nd Offense:</strong> Removal from alliance or <strong>NAP Blacklist</strong>.<br>
<strong>Blacklist Policy:</strong> Prohibits joining any NAP-protected alliance.</p>
